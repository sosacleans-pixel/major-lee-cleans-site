FAQ

Common questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve the Salt Lake and Wasatch area. Not sure if you are in range? Send us a note and we will let you know.

How do I get a quote?

Email majorleecleans@gmail.com with the type of clean you want, the size of your home and your ZIP code. We will reply with a quote.

How much does a cleaning cost?

Every home is different, so we quote based on size, condition and the type of clean. Request a quote and we will get back to you.

Do you offer recurring cleaning?

Yes. Recurring cleaning is one of our core services. Let us know how often you would like a visit when you request a quote.
